  <h1>Bio</h1>
  <p class="bio-intro">William (Yang) Xu is a researcher studying decision-making and performance evaluation in modern AI systems.</p>
  <p>He earned his PhD in Industrial Engineering from <a href="https://engineering.purdue.edu/IE">Purdue University</a> in 2026, advised by <a href="https://web.ics.purdue.edu/~vaneet/">Vaneet Aggarwal</a>. He also holds an M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Purdue University (2025) and a B.E. in Electrical Engineering from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (2022).</p>
  <p>In summer 2026, he was an Applied Scientist Intern at Amazon, where he worked on generative AI.</p>
  <p>He received the NeurIPS 2025 Financial Aid Award and the NeurIPS 2026 Top Reviewer Award.</p>
